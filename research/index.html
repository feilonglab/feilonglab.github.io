Research at the Feilong Lab

Our research spans multiple themes in computational and cognitive neuroscience. We focus on decoding and predicting individualized brain responses and linking individual differences in brain organization to behavior and cognition. To achieve this, we develop and refine advanced tools for neuroimaging data analysis, including hyperalignment and brain templates. We also examine how similar functions are implemented across different species and systems, from humans and non-human primates to artificial neural networks.
